# Privacy Information & Data Handling Policy

Information on how data is handled, stored, and protected within **InvoiceFine**.

---

## 1. Local-First Data Storage

InvoiceFine is designed with an offline-first architecture:

- **Local Storage**: Your business profile, product catalog, customer records, invoice history, and transaction ledgers are stored locally on your device.
- **No Mandatory Cloud Account**: You are not required to create an online cloud account or transmit your private sales data to an external server to generate everyday bills.
- **User Ownership**: Your financial and customer records remain strictly under your control on your own hardware.

---

## 2. Data Synchronization & Backups

- **Local Backup Archives**: InvoiceFine enables you to generate local backup files on demand. These files can be saved to your local storage, external USB drives, or transferred between your Android and Linux desktop devices.
- **Third-Party Cloud Backup (Optional)**: If you choose to enable cloud backup services (such as Google Drive backup), data transfer occurs directly between your device and your chosen cloud provider account using their official APIs.

---

## 3. Subscriptions & Payments

- On Android, in-app subscription purchases are processed securely through Google Play In-App Billing. InvoiceFine does not store or process your credit card, debit card, or banking authentication credentials.
- Financial transactions for subscription purchases are governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

---

## 4. Device Permissions (Mobile App)

When running on Android, InvoiceFine may request specific permissions strictly to enable corresponding user-initiated features:
- **Bluetooth**: Required to discover, pair with, and send print commands to portable wireless thermal receipt printers.
- **Camera**: Required strictly for optical barcode scanning of inventory items during checkout.
- **Storage / Media Access**: Required to save generated PDF invoices, export database backups, or select business logo images from your gallery.

No personal data from your camera, photos, or contacts is read or transmitted without explicit user action.
