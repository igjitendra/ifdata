## 🎯 How the Team Should Use This Library

To create content for any feature in under 10 minutes:

```
Step 1: Choose Feature
        ↓ Go to FEATURE-CONTENT-MATRIX.md to find your topic (e.g., Stock Ledger or Thermal Printing)
Step 2: Select Format
        ↓ Choose Single Post (SINGLE-POST-PROMPTS.md), Carousel (CAROUSEL-PROMPTS.md), or Reel (REELS-PROMPTS.md)
Step 3: Generate Visuals
        ↓ Pick an AI Image Prompt (IMAGE-PROMPTS.md) or Video Prompt (VIDEO-PROMPTS.md)
Step 4: Composite Real UI
        ↓ Insert real screenshots from assets/screenshots/ into the generated device mockup
Step 5: Finalize Copy
        ↓ Grab matching hooks, captions, and hashtags from CAPTIONS.md and HASHTAGS.md
Step 6: Publish & Track
        ↓ Follow the 30-day publishing rhythm in CONTENT-CALENDAR.md
```

## 🎨 Brand Identity Guidelines

- **Primary Brand Color**: `#EF3035` (Energetic Crimson / InvoiceFine Red)
- **Primary Text Color**: `#171717` (Deep Slate Charcoal)
- **Background Tones**: Clean whites, crisp neutral grays, warm paper whites
- **Brand Personality**: Practical, dependable, modern, trustworthy, straightforward
- **Visual Rule**: Avoid flashy crypto/fintech neon, exaggerated 3D bubbles, or cluttered dashboards. InvoiceFine represents an honest, robust tool for real Indian shopkeepers and small business owners.

---

## 📱 The Real UI Compositing Rule

1. **Never generate fake app screens via AI**: AI image and video generators hallucinate buttons, text, and features.
2. **Device Mockup Workflow**: Use AI prompts to generate realistic Indian shop counters, business owners, desks, and holding devices with clean, blank screens.
3. **Insert Verified Screenshots**: Place real screenshots from [`assets/screenshots/`](assets/screenshots/) directly onto device screens using Canva, Photoshop, or Figma.
