## ⚠️ The Real UI Composite Rule

> **CRITICAL PRODUCTION DIRECTIVE**:  
> **Never ask the AI image generator to draw or render the InvoiceFine software interface.** AI models hallucinate illegible text, fake buttons, and imaginary features.  
>  
> Always generate the **commercial background, realistic Indian shopkeeper/merchant character, store environment, and hand-held device mockup with a clean, blank screen**.  
> In your final design tool (Canva, Figma, Photoshop), composite the authentic high-resolution screenshot from [`assets/screenshots/`](../assets/screenshots/) into the blank device screen.

## 🎨 Post-Production Compositing Guide for Designers

When using the generated images above in Figma or Canva:
1. **Mockup Screen Fitting**: Use the *Perspective Crop* or *Smart Mockup* tool to fit the real screenshot from [`assets/screenshots/`](../assets/screenshots/) directly into the blank device area.
2. **Add Primary Brand Accent**: Use InvoiceFine Red (`#EF3035`) for headline banners, pill badges, and arrow callouts.
3. **Typography Standard**: Use modern sans-serif fonts such as **Inter**, **Outfit**, or **Poppins** for crisp legibility on mobile screens.
4. **Export Specs**: Export at 100% quality in PNG format for crystal-clear social feeds.
