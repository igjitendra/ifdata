2. **Select Format**: Match the idea to [SINGLE-POST-PROMPTS.md](SINGLE-POST-PROMPTS.md), [CAROUSEL-PROMPTS.md](CAROUSEL-PROMPTS.md), or [REELS-PROMPTS.md](REELS-PROMPTS.md).
3. **Pull Real Visual Assets**: Check [`assets/screenshots/`](../assets/screenshots/) for the authentic screenshot matching that feature.
4. **Draft Copy**: Use [CAPTIONS.md](CAPTIONS.md) and [HASHTAGS.md](HASHTAGS.md) for final distribution.
