## ⏱️ Standard Reel Timeline Blueprint

```
0–2 sec   → [HOOK] Stop the scroll with a relatable pain point or unexpected visual.
2–5 sec   → [PROBLEM] Agitate the friction (wasted time, lost money, messy paper).
5–10 sec  → [SOLUTION] Introduce InvoiceFine with a clean, smooth transition.
10–15 sec → [DEMO] Show authentic feature in action (composite real app capture).
15–20 sec → [CTA] Clear, direct instruction to download or follow.
```
