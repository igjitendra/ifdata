# InvoiceFine Carousel Templates & Slide Guides

Multi-slide educational and conversion-focused carousel frameworks (Instagram & LinkedIn Document Carousels).

---

## 🎨 Design Rules for High-Save Carousels

1. **Aspect Ratio**: 4:5 Portrait (1080 × 1350 px) for maximum mobile viewport coverage.
2. **Color Palette**:
   - Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Paper (`#F9F9FB`)
   - Accent Pill / Callout: Crimson Red (`#EF3035`)
   - Primary Text: Deep Slate (`#171717`)
   - Secondary Text: Muted Charcoal (`#52525B`)
3. **Typography**: Bold headline (60–72pt), clean body copy (28–34pt).
4. **Slide Indicator**: Number pills in top-right corner (e.g., `1 / 7`).
5. **Swipe Trigger**: Subtle arrow or "Swipe →" micro-cue on slides 1–6.
