## 📅 Monthly Distribution Strategy

- **Reels / Shorts (Vertical Video)**: 8 Posts (High viral reach & visual proof)
- **Carousels (Multi-slide Guides)**: 8 Posts (High saves, algorithmic authority & education)
- **Single-Image Posts**: 10 Posts (Direct feature spotlights, quick tips, announcements)
- **Community / Discussion Stories**: 4 Posts (Customer polls, Q&A, behind-the-scenes)
