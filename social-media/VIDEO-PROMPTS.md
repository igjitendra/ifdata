# InvoiceFine AI Video Generation Prompt Library

A production-ready library of AI video prompts optimized for tools like **Runway Gen-3 Alpha**, **Kling AI**, **Luma Dream Machine**, and **Sora**.

---

## 🎬 The Video Compositing Directive

> **PRODUCTION MANDATE**:  
> Generative video models cannot reliably render dynamic UI interactions or crisp text on screens.  
>  
> **The Two-Layer Production Workflow**:  
> 1. **Base Layer (AI Video)**: Generate cinematic live-action footage of real Indian shopkeepers, customer handovers, thermal printers dispensing paper, or bustling counter environments with blank screens on devices.  
> 2. **Composite Layer (Real Screen Recording)**: Capture authentic screen recordings of InvoiceFine in action (from phone or Linux desktop) and track/composite the footage onto the device screen in After Effects, Premiere Pro, or CapCut.

## Video 1: Cinematic Product Launch (30 Seconds)
- **Objective**: High-impact brand awareness introducing InvoiceFine across mobile and desktop.
- **Duration**: 30 seconds
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (1080 × 1920) or 16:9 (1920 × 1080)
- **Target Audience**: Ambitious small retail and wholesale entrepreneurs across India.

### Scene Breakdown
- **Scene 1 (0–6s)**: Close-up tracking shot across a bustling morning Indian marketplace, shifting focus to a confident shopkeeper unlocking his storefront with a warm smile.
- **Scene 2 (6–12s)**: Medium shot at the checkout counter. The shopkeeper smoothly scans a product barcode with his smartphone. Next to him, a portable Bluetooth printer instantly begins printing.
- **Scene 3 (12–18s)**: Smooth cut to a modern office/desktop workstation. The merchant operates InvoiceFine Desktop on a Linux PC, clicking on an item to inspect the live stock ledger.
- **Scene 4 (18–24s)**: Customer perspective: A happy shopper receives a crisp printed receipt with a store logo, and simultaneously hears a gentle WhatsApp notification chime on their phone.
- **Scene 5 (24–30s)**: Sleek graphic end card with InvoiceFine logo, Crimson (`#EF3035`) brand pill, and direct download badge.

### Technical Generation Prompt (Runway / Kling / Luma)
```text
Prompt:
Cinematic 4K commercial footage. Slow push-in shot of a confident 35-year-old Indian male store owner standing behind a neat, organized retail shop counter. He holds a modern smartphone smoothly toward the camera, screen displaying a flat solid green surface for screen replacement. Warm morning sunlight streaming through storefront glass, natural golden-hour tone, shallow depth of field, 35mm cinema lens, 24fps, photorealistic textures, zero distortion in hands and face.

Negative Prompt:
jittery motion, sudden warping, morphing hands, distorted phone, blurry faces, low frame rate, artificial plastic look, cartoonish lighting
```

- **Camera Movement**: Slow, steady forward dolly tracking shot.
- **On-Screen Text**:
  - *0:04*: Your Complete Business Manager

  - *0:20*: Works on Mobile & Linux Desktop
