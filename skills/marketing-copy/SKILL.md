# InvoiceFine Marketing Copywriting Skill

## Purpose
Craft high-converting headlines, curiosity hooks, value propositions, body copy, and calls-to-action that speak directly to Indian shop owners without corporate fluff or generic AI buzzwords.

## When to Use
- Writing headlines for banners, ads, posters, and flyers.
- Drafting website landing page hero sections.
- Writing promotional email blasts and direct WhatsApp broadcast copy.

## Inputs
- Offering/Feature (e.g. Offline Invoicing, Thermal Printing, Digital Khata).
- Copy placement (Ad, Hero section, Social card, Print flyer).
- Language (English, Hinglish, Hindi).

## Workflow
1. Identify the high-friction pain point experienced by the merchant.
2. Formulate 3 distinct hook variations:
   - **Direct Benefit Hook**: Clear, outcome-focused ("Print shop receipts in 2 seconds").
   - **Pain/Loss Aversion Hook**: Highlight cost of inaction ("How much money did you lose in unrecorded customer credit this month?").
   - **Curiosity/Contrast Hook**: Counter-intuitive comparison ("Why top retailers replaced heavy desktop computers with a simple smartphone app").
3. Write concise, punchy body text (under 40 words) articulating the exact mechanism of solution.
4. Conclude with an unambiguous, action-oriented call to action.

## Rules
- Strictly avoid generic AI clichés: "Revolutionize your business", "Game changer", "Take your business to the next level", "Supercharge your growth".
- Replace abstractions with concrete retail metrics: "Save 1 hour every evening", "Bill in 5 seconds", "Track every rupee of Udhaar".
- Write in short, readable sentences with active voice.

## Output Format
- **PRIMARY HEADLINE**: Bold, scannable title (under 8 words).
- **SUBHEADLINE**: Supporting benefit statement (10–15 words).
- **BODY COPY**: Concise value paragraph or 3 bullet points.
- **CTA BUTTON TEXT**: Clear action (e.g., "Download Free", "Try Offline Billing Now").

## Examples
- *Headline*: "Your Shop Counter Deserves Faster Billing"
- *Subheadline*: "Create GST bills, print thermal receipts, and track customer dues 100% offline."
- *Body*: "Say goodbye to slow paper bills and manual tax math. InvoiceFine lets you bill in 5 seconds from your phone—even when your internet is completely down."
- *CTA*: "Download InvoiceFine Free • Zero Setup Fees"

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Free of banned clichés (revolutionize, game changer, next level).
- [ ] Quantifiable or concrete benefit presented.
- [ ] Active voice and simple vocabulary.
- [ ] Compelling, frictionless CTA.
