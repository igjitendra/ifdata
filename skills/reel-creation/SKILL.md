# InvoiceFine Instagram Reel & YouTube Short Creation Skill

## Purpose
Produce viral, high-retention short-form vertical video scripts (15 to 30 seconds) for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts combining pattern-interrupt hooks, fast visual pacing, authentic merchant audio, and real feature demos.

## When to Use
- Creating top-of-funnel short video scripts.
- Producing relatable shopkeeper skits and counter-work hacks.
- Rapidly demonstrating a high-value software capability (e.g. 5-second billing).

## Inputs
- Feature to feature (e.g. Barcode Scanning, Thermal Receipt Printing, Customer Khata).
- Audience persona (Kirana, Mobile Repair, Garments, Hardware).
- Language / Tone (Hinglish conversational, English snappy, or Hindi natural).

## Workflow
1. **0–3 Seconds (The Hook)**: Visual pattern interrupt + bold spoken hook addressing a costly retail headache.
2. **3–8 Seconds (The Friction/Tension)**: Highlight the awkwardness, lost money, or slow customer line of traditional manual methods.
3. **8–18 Seconds (The Solution & Real Demo)**: Show the merchant performing the action inside InvoiceFine in 2 taps; show the physical result (e.g. receipt prints, WhatsApp message arrives).
4. **18–25 Seconds (The Core Benefit)**: Reiterate the key payoff (e.g. "Save 45 minutes every evening", "Never lose a single rupee").
5. **25–30 Seconds (The Call-to-Action)**: Bold on-screen text and spoken CTA directing viewer to the bio link or app store.

## Rules
- First 3 seconds must contain both audio and text hook to capture sound-off viewers.
- Keep total script length under 75 spoken words for 25–30 second videos.
- Never use fake download counts or artificial claims like "Ranked #1 App in India".

## Output Format
- **REEL TITLE & CONCEPT**
- **HOOK TEXT & AUDIO (0-3s)**
- **COVER IMAGE TEXT**: High-CTR thumbnail copy.
- **SCENE-BY-SCENE SCRIPT**:
  - `Time`: Second range
  - `Visual`: Actor action and props
  - `On-Screen Text`: Exact typography overlay
  - `Voice-over`: Natural spoken dialogue
- **REAL SCREEN RECORDING OVERLAY**: Screenshot/screen record asset reference
- **CAPTION & HASHTAGS**: Ready-to-publish text block
