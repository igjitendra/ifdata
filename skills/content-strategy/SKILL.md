# InvoiceFine Content Strategy Skill

## Purpose
Structure marketing campaigns and editorial calendars around proven conversion funnels, content pillars, and retail audience personas.

## When to Use
- Planning weekly or 30-day content schedules.
- Deciding which content format best serves a marketing goal.
- Segmenting messaging for different business sizes and retail sectors.

## Inputs
- Business goal (Brand Awareness, User Acquisition, Feature Adoption, Pro Retention).
- Target retail vertical (Kirana, Garments, Electronics, Hardware, Wholesaler).
- Timeline or campaign cadence (Daily, Weekly, Launch).

## Workflow
1. Map the request to one of four core content pillars:
   - **Pillar 1: Value & Education** (GST guides, margin calculations, inventory tips).
   - **Pillar 2: Problem & Solution** (Lost credit dues, slow billing queues, paper Khata headaches).
   - **Pillar 3: Product & Feature Spotlight** (Barcode scanning, 58mm thermal receipts, multi-item invoicing).
   - **Pillar 4: Proof & Trust** (Real UI demonstrations, offline security, clean accounting).
2. Assign the optimal format:
   - Awareness: 15–30s Reels, Short Videos, Memes/Relatable skits.
   - Consideration: 7–10 slide educational carousels, problem-solution graphics.
   - Conversion: Step-by-step product demos, feature spotlights with direct download CTAs.
3. Formulate the publishing rhythm and conversion path.

## Rules
- Maintain balanced pillar distribution: 40% Value/Education, 30% Problem/Solution, 20% Product/Feature, 10% Trust/Conversion.
- Never publish repetitive promotional messages back-to-back.
- Ensure every piece of content provides standalone value to the merchant even before they download the app.

## Output Format
- **Campaign Objective**: Target marketing outcome.
- **Content Pillar**: Assigned strategic pillar.
- **Funnel Stage**: Top (Awareness), Middle (Consideration), or Bottom (Conversion).
- **Format Recommendation**: Reel, Carousel, Single Image, or Video Demo.
- **Conversion CTA**: Specific next step for the viewer.

## Examples
- *Top of Funnel*: 15s Reel highlighting the frustration of calculating 18% GST by hand during a store rush.
- *Middle of Funnel*: 7-slide Carousel explaining how to calculate real gross margins after operating expenses.
- *Bottom of Funnel*: 30s product demo showing instant Bluetooth thermal receipt printing with download link.

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Aligns with business goals and content pillars.
- [ ] Format matches the user's attention span on the target channel.
- [ ] Provides actionable retail value.
- [ ] Clear call-to-action included.
