# InvoiceFine Hindi & Hinglish Content Writing Skill

## Purpose
Guide the AI agent in writing natural, authentic, and culturally resonant Hindi and Hinglish marketing copy that mirrors how Indian small business owners actually communicate at their retail counters.

## When to Use
- Drafting social media posts, Reels, and ads targeted at Indian regional shopkeepers.
- Writing voice-over scripts for short video ads and product walkthroughs.
- Creating customer WhatsApp reminder templates and promotional broadcast messages.

## Inputs
- Content format (Reel script, Carousel, Caption, Ad copy).
- Primary topic or feature.
- Target demographic (Kirana owner, wholesale trader, mobile repair shopkeeper).

## Workflow
1. Select appropriate dialect register:
   - **Conversational Hinglish**: Roman script blending common Hindi phrasing with familiar English business terms (e.g., *Bill, Print, Stock, Payment, Dashboard, Customer*). Preferred for Instagram Reels and WhatsApp.
   - **Clean Devanagari Hindi**: Natural, easy-to-read Devanagari script. Preferred for formal print flyers, localized YouTube titles, and regional Facebook posts.
2. Avoid literal or machine translations:
   - *Machine Translation (Bad)*: "अपनी सूची का प्रबंधन करें और अपनी बिक्री बढ़ाएं"
   - *Natural Phrasing (Good)*: "दुकान का स्टॉक और हिसाब रखें हमेशा उंगलियों पर"
3. Keep sentence structure rhythmic, friendly, and respectful.

## Rules
- Never use heavy, archaic Sanskritized vocabulary that merchants never use in real life.
- Keep English technical words in their familiar form (use *Bill*, *Receipt*, *GST*, *Stock*, *Bluetooth* rather than obscure translated terms).
- Ensure Hinglish spelling is consistent and phonetic.

## Output Format
- **HINGLISH COPY**: Conversational Roman script with natural flow.
- **HINDI COPY**: Clear Devanagari script with everyday commercial vocabulary.
- **ENGLISH MEANING SUMMARY**: Brief English translation for verification.

## Examples
### Reel Hook & Script (Hinglish)
```text
HOOK: "Bhaiya, pichhle mahine ka hisaab kab clear karoge?"
BODY: "Har baar customer se udhaar maangne mein sharm aati hai?
Ab sharmaane ki zaroorat nahi! InvoiceFine ke Customer Ledger se ek tap mein bhejo polite WhatsApp reminder.
Bina kisi behas ke, pura hisaab customer ke phone par!"
CTA: "Aaj hi download karein InvoiceFine free!"
```

### Banner Copy (Hindi Devanagari)
```text
HEADLINE: दुकान का बिलिंग और खाता, अब सिर्फ एक ऐप में!
SUBHEADLINE: बिना इंटरनेट के 100% ऑफलाइन काम करता है। आज ही डाउनलोड करें।
CTA: फ्री में डाउनलोड करें
```

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Phrasing sounds like natural merchant dialogue.
- [ ] Common business terms (bill, stock, printer) kept in familiar language.
- [ ] No clumsy machine translations or overly stiff formal grammar.
- [ ] Tone is polite, respectful, and encouraging.
