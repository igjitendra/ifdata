# InvoiceFine Multi-Platform Social Media Skill

## Purpose
Adapt core marketing concepts, hooks, aspect ratios, and copy styles across different social media channels (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, and WhatsApp Business).

## When to Use
- Translating a single feature concept into tailored posts for multiple platforms.
- Choosing optimal media dimensions and video durations.
- Crafting platform-specific captions and engagement mechanisms.

## Inputs
- Feature or topic.
- Target platforms (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, WhatsApp).
- Desired format (Single Post, Video, Carousel, Story).

## Workflow
1. Identify primary platform constraints and user behaviors:
   - **Instagram**: Visual-first, snappy hooks (0–3s), vertical reels (9:16) or clean feed graphics (4:5 / 1:1).
   - **Facebook**: Community-focused, relatable local merchant scenarios, shareable retail advice, feed link previews.
   - **YouTube Shorts**: High-speed visual hook, clear pacing, continuous action, pinned comment download links.
   - **LinkedIn**: Professional tone, business profitability, B2B wholesale workflows, PDF slide documents.
   - **WhatsApp Business**: Direct, personal, clean formatting with bold bullet points, clear immediate CTA.
2. Adjust headline, aspect ratio, tone, and character count to suit each channel.
3. Deliver native platform copy rather than copying identical text everywhere.

## Rules
- Match aspect ratios to platforms: 9:16 for Reels/Shorts/Stories, 4:5 or 1:1 for Instagram/Facebook feed, 16:9 for YouTube.
- Never use hashtags on WhatsApp Business. Limit hashtags on LinkedIn to 3–5 professional tags.
- Keep Instagram captions scannable with bullet points and line breaks.

## Output Format
- **Platform Deliverable**: Tailored post copy, recommended aspect ratio, duration, and CTA for each requested channel.

## Examples
- *Instagram Reel*: Fast-paced vertical hook ("Stop losing Rs 5,000 every month in forgotten Udhaar") with dynamic captions.
- *LinkedIn Post*: Professional case study breakdown ("How independent Indian retailers are cutting counter checkout time by 60% using local offline POS software").

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Aspect ratios match platform standards.
- [ ] Tone and vocabulary match the specific platform audience.
- [ ] CTA and links properly placed (e.g. bio link for IG, comment/description for YouTube).
