# InvoiceFine Hashtag Strategy Skill

## Purpose
Generate targeted, high-intent, and spam-free hashtag clusters grouped by retail vertical, feature focus, and geographic relevance to maximize organic reach.

## When to Use
- Finalizing social media posts for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or YouTube Shorts.
- Categorizing content for platform search algorithms.
- Avoiding shadowbans caused by overused, banned, or irrelevant spam hashtags.

## Inputs
- Content topic or feature (e.g. GST Billing, Thermal Printing, Kirana Inventory).
- Target platform (Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube).

## Workflow
1. Select 1–2 Brand Anchor tags: `#InvoiceFine #ProCSCTools`.
2. Select 2–3 Feature-Specific tags: e.g. `#GSTBilling #ThermalPrinter #KhataBookAlternative`.
3. Select 2–3 Retail / Vertical tags: e.g. `#KiranaStore #RetailIndia #IndianBusiness`.
4. Select 1–2 Discovery / Problem tags: e.g. `#SmallBusinessTips #BillingSoftware`.
5. Assemble into a compact group of 8 to 12 total hashtags.

## Rules
- Never use spammy, irrelevant vanity tags: `#love #followme #instagood #like4like`.
- Do not exceed 10–12 hashtags on Instagram; do not exceed 3–5 on LinkedIn.
- Group hashtags neatly at the bottom of the caption or in the first comment.

## Output Format
- **PRIMARY BRAND CLUSTER**
- **FEATURE-SPECIFIC CLUSTER**
- **VERTICAL-SPECIFIC CLUSTER (Kirana / Retail / Tech)**
- **COMBINED PUBLISHING BLOCK**

## Examples
### Cluster for Thermal Printing & POS
```text
#InvoiceFine #ThermalPrinting #BluetoothPrinter #POSBilling #RetailSoftware #ShopManagement #KiranaStore #SmallBusinessIndia #RetailBilling
```

### Cluster for GST Billing & Tax
```text
#InvoiceFine #GSTBilling #GSTIndia #TaxInvoicing #B2BBilling #AccountingSoftware #Vyapar #IndianRetailer #ShopkeeperLife
```

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Total tag count is within safe limits (8–12 on IG, 3–5 on LinkedIn).
- [ ] No generic vanity or spam tags included.
- [ ] Every tag directly relates to the post content and target audience.
