# InvoiceFine Social Caption Writing Skill

## Purpose
Write engaging, high-retention social media captions in English, Hinglish, and Hindi tailored for Instagram feeds, Facebook posts, Reels, and carousels.

## When to Use
- Finalizing post copy for social media publishing.
- Creating multilingual variants of promotional messages.
- Structuring caption layouts for mobile readability.

## Inputs
- Content format (Single Post, Carousel, Reel, Story).
- Feature or topic.
- Language preference (English, Hinglish, Hindi).

## Workflow
1. Write the **Opening Hook Line**: Keep it under 100 characters so it appears completely before the Instagram "more..." cutoff.
2. Structure the **Body Paragraph**: Use 2–3 short sentences or clear bullet points with clean spacing.
3. Formulate the **Call to Action**: Tell the reader exactly what to do (e.g., "Tap the link in our bio to download free", "Save this post for your store").
4. Attach relevant, non-spam hashtags (max 5–10 high-relevance tags).

## Rules
- Always insert clean line breaks between thoughts.
- In Hinglish, write natural colloquial phrases that merchants actually speak; avoid stiff textbook translations.
- In Hindi, use simple, friendly vocabulary commonly understood in Indian markets.

## Output Format
- **CAPTION VARIANT 1: Short / Reel Caption** (Snappy, under 30 words)
- **CAPTION VARIANT 2: Educational / Carousel Caption** (Structured, bulleted, 50–80 words)
- **CAPTION VARIANT 3: Sales / Promotional Caption** (Benefit-driven with immediate CTA)
