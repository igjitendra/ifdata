## Category 1: Billing & Invoicing

### 1. GST Billing
- **What it does**: Creates tax-compliant invoices with automatic calculation of CGST, SGST, and IGST based on customer state and item HSN codes.
- **Who needs it**: GST-registered businesses, wholesalers, retailers selling taxable goods.
- **Problem it solves**: Manual tax calculation errors and fear of filing incorrect tax returns.
- **User benefit**: 100% accurate tax math in seconds, saving hours during monthly GST filing.
- **Content angles**: Educational (GST vs Non-GST), Problem/Solution (Calculation headache resolved), Feature demo (3-tap invoice creation).
- **Demo angle**: Adding 2 taxable items, showing automatic intra-state 50/50 CGST/SGST split, and tapping finalize.
- **Visual concept**: Shopkeeper on smartphone with thermal receipt emerging showing clear GST breakdown.

### 3. CGST / SGST Automated Split
- **What it does**: Automatically divides applicable GST into Central GST and State GST for intra-state sales.
- **Who needs it**: All merchants selling within their state.
- **Problem it solves**: Forgetting tax split ratios (e.g., 9% + 9% on 18% items).
- **User benefit**: Guaranteed compliance with state tax norms without manual calculations.
- **Content angles**: "Never calculate 2.5%, 6%, or 9% by hand again."
- **Demo angle**: Live calculation animation showing single 18% rate cleanly splitting into 9% CGST and 9% SGST.
- **Visual concept**: Clean split-screen graphic contrasting manual calculator scribbles with digital precision.
- **CTA**: Automate Your Tax Math • Download InvoiceFine

### 4. IGST Inter-State Calculation
- **What it does**: Detects out-of-state customer GSTINs and applies full Integrated GST in a single line.
- **Who needs it**: Wholesalers, traders, and suppliers shipping goods across state borders.
- **Problem it solves**: Applying wrong tax headers to out-of-state dispatches resulting in tax penalties.
- **User benefit**: Confident interstate billing that matches GST portal standards.
- **Content angles**: Interstate shipping compliance made effortless.
- **Demo angle**: Selecting buyer in a different state; app automatically shifts to single IGST column.
- **Visual concept**: Dispatch counter with boxed goods ready for transport, accompanied by professional IGST bill.
- **CTA**: Ship Pan-India with Compliant Billing • Get InvoiceFine

### 5. Live GSTIN Search & Verification
- **What it does**: Validates customer GSTIN format and structure directly within the customer setup form.
- **Who needs it**: B2B sellers and wholesalers issuing formal tax credits.
- **Problem it solves**: Typing errors in buyer GSTIN causing ITC (Input Tax Credit) rejection for clients.
- **User benefit**: Guarantees your B2B customers receive their input tax credits without friction.
- **Content angles**: Build client trust with flawless B2B invoices.
