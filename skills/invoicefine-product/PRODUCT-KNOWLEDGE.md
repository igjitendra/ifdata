# InvoiceFine Product Knowledge Base
# PRODUCT-KNOWLEDGE.md

This document provides verified, public-facing product knowledge for InvoiceFine.

---

## 1. Product Overview & Positioning

- **Product Name**: InvoiceFine
- **Core Value Proposition**: Complete Business Management from Your Phone and Desktop.
- **Tagline**: *Bill banao. Stock track karo. Payment manage karo. Business samjho.*
- **Target Audience**: Micro, small, and medium businesses in India, including retail shops, grocery (Kirana) stores, electronics dealers, garment retailers, hardware merchants, wholesalers, and independent service providers.

---

## 2. Platforms & Hardware Support

### Platforms
1. **Android Mobile Application**: Full-featured billing, customer management, inventory, and expense tracking directly from a smartphone or tablet.
2. **Linux Desktop Software**: Fast counter POS billing, keyboard-driven navigation, barcode scanning, and large-screen reporting.

### Hardware Integrations
- **Thermal Receipt Printers**: 58mm (2-inch) and 80mm (3-inch) support.
- **Connectivity Types**: Bluetooth mobile printers, Wi-Fi network printers, USB OTG thermal printers, and Android system print.
- **Office Printing**: Standard A4 invoices in 5 professional layout templates (Modern, Classic, Professional, Compact, Premium).
- **Barcode Scanners**: Built-in smartphone camera scanner + USB/Bluetooth plug-and-play handheld barcode guns.

---

## 3. Core Business Problems Solved

| Problem | How InvoiceFine Solves It |
|---|---|
| **Slow manual billing & calculation errors** | Generates GST and Non-GST bills in under 10 seconds with automated tax math (CGST, SGST, IGST). |
| **Lost money from forgotten customer dues (Udhaar)** | Digital customer ledger tracks every credit transaction, partial payment, and outstanding balance with WhatsApp reminders. |
| **Stockouts and unexpected inventory loss** | Real-time stock decrement on sales, purchase stock-in tracking, low stock alerts, and full inventory ledgers. |
| **Internet downtime disrupting retail counters** | 100% offline-first architecture. Invoices, stock records, and reports function seamlessly without internet. |
| **Expensive software subscriptions** | Transparent free tier with generous daily limits, paired with an affordable one-time or pro upgrade. |

---

## 4. Key Functional Modules

1. **Billing & Tax**:
   - GST compliant invoices with state code selection and automated intra-state / inter-state tax split.
   - Non-GST and simplified retail cash memo formats.
   - Live GSTIN search and validation.
2. **Customer & Khata Management**:
   - Contact book integration, transaction ledger, payment history, and one-tap WhatsApp payment reminders.
3. **Products & Services Catalog**:
   - Product categories, purchase vs. selling price tracking, wholesale pricing tiers, HSN/SAC codes, and barcode tags.
4. **Expense Tracking**:
   - Track rent, electricity, packaging, logistics, and staff salaries to calculate true net business profit.
5. **Business Analytics & Reports**:
   - Daily sales summaries, top-selling items, expense breakdowns, profit & loss, and GST filing summaries.
6. **Data Privacy & Backup**:
   - All records are saved locally on the merchant's device. Supports encrypted Google Drive backup and manual local JSON/CSV export.
