# InvoiceFine Multi-Slide Carousel Design Skill

## Purpose
Design high-retention, educational, and problem-solving multi-slide carousels (5, 7, 8, or 10 slides) for Instagram and LinkedIn that guide merchants from problem identification to feature solution and conversion.

## When to Use
- Breaking down multi-step retail workflows (e.g. GST calculation, digital Khata management).
- Explaining complex business concepts (e.g. true profit margins, inventory dead stock).
- Highlighting feature benefits through structured swipeable narratives.

## Inputs
- Topic or Feature (e.g. Customer Ledger, Stock Management).
- Slide count (Standard is 7 slides; deep-dive is 10 slides).
- Target audience (Kirana, Retail, Hardware, Wholesaler).
- Language (English, Hinglish, Hindi).

## Workflow
1. Select carousel architecture:
   - **Slide 1**: High-curiosity Hook (problem callout or counter-intuitive insight).
   - **Slide 2**: Agitate the Problem (describe daily friction, lost revenue, or wasted time).
   - **Slide 3**: The Root Cause (why manual paper methods or old desktop software fail).
   - **Slide 4**: The Solution Principle (the smart way to manage records).
   - **Slide 5**: The Feature in Action (how InvoiceFine handles the task in 2 taps).
   - **Slide 6**: Tangible Merchant Benefit (exact time or money saved).
   - **Slide 7**: Direct CTA (Download link, swipe for bio, comment for guide).
2. Write concise copy per slide: Limit headlines to 5–7 words and body text to 20–30 words.
3. Define visual concept and screenshot requirement for each slide.

## Rules
- Avoid wall-of-text slides. Each slide must be readable in under 3 seconds.
- Every carousel must feature at least one real screenshot mockup from `assets/screenshots/`.
- Maintain consistent visual branding: white card background, crimson `#EF3035` focal accents, clean dark charcoal `#171717` typography.

## Output Format
- **CAROUSEL TITLE & OBJECTIVE**
- **TOTAL SLIDES**: e.g., 7 Slides
- **SLIDE-BY-SLIDE BREAKDOWN**:
  - `SLIDE [N]`: Purpose (Hook / Problem / Solution / Demo / Benefit / CTA)
  - `HEADLINE`: Punchy slide title
  - `BODY TEXT`: 2–3 short sentences
  - `VISUAL CONCEPT`: Graphic layout, icon, or device mockup
  - `REAL SCREENSHOT INSTRUCTION`: Exact asset to insert (if applicable)

## Examples
```text
CAROUSEL: How to Recover 100% of Shop Customer Dues (7 Slides)

SLIDE 1: HOOK
- Headline: "Losing Rs 10,000 every month in forgotten Udhaar?"
- Body: "80% of retail shopkeepers lose money not because customers refuse to pay, but because both sides forget."
- Visual: Close-up of messy, ink-stained paper Khata notebook with question mark.

SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM
- Headline: "Paper Khata Diary Has 3 Costly Flaws"
- Body: "1. Torn pages. 2. Disputed calculations. 3. Zero automated reminders."
- Visual: Flat vector illustration of 3 warning icons with clean typography.

SLIDE 5: THE INVOICEFINE SOLUTION
- Headline: "Customer Ledger + 1-Tap WhatsApp Reminder"
- Body: "Record partial payments, track live balances, and send polite WhatsApp reminders in 5 seconds."
- Visual: Phone mockup showing Customer Ledger screen.
- Screenshot: assets/screenshots/mobile/mobile-screen-2.png

SLIDE 7: CTA
- Headline: "Take Control of Your Shop Dues Today"
- Body: "Download InvoiceFine free. Works 100% offline with zero monthly fees."
- Visual: Clean brand card with InvoiceFine logo and Google Play badge.
```

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Hook creates immediate relevance for the merchant.
- [ ] Word count per slide is scannable on mobile.
- [ ] Real screenshot mockup assigned to demonstration slide.
- [ ] Clear call-to-action on the closing slide.
