# InvoiceFine Retail Business Education Skill

## Purpose
Create practical, highly valuable educational content for Indian merchants regarding GST compliance, gross profit calculations, stock management, and cash flow control without presenting unauthorized legal or tax advice.

## When to Use
- Writing educational social media posts, blog articles, and video scripts.
- Explaining GST slab rates, HSN codes, and input tax credit (ITC) principles.
- Helping small business owners understand working capital and expense management.

## Inputs
- Educational topic (e.g. GST Split, Dead Stock Reduction, Customer Credit Management).
- Target audience (Small retailer, Kirana owner, Wholesaler).

## Workflow
1. Identify a common merchant misconception or operational blind spot (e.g. confusing sales revenue with net profit).
2. Explain the concept using simple, relatable Indian retail examples (e.g., selling Rs 10,000 of goods with Rs 2,000 in hidden shop expenses).
3. Provide an actionable 3-step practical rule the shopkeeper can implement today.
4. Show how InvoiceFine automates or simplifies this rule.
5. Append standard educational disclaimer.

## Rules
- **LEGAL / TAX DISCLAIMER MANDATORY**: Never claim to be a Chartered Accountant, tax authority, or government representative. Clearly state: *"This content is for educational purposes only. For official tax filings, consult a certified tax professional."*
- Ensure tax formulas (CGST + SGST = GST) are 100% mathematically accurate.
- Keep financial math simple and easy to follow on mobile screens.

## Output Format
- **LESSON TITLE**: Actionable educational topic.
- **THE RETAIL REALITY**: Relatable merchant scenario.
- **THE 3-STEP SYSTEM**: Practical, clear advice.
- **HOW INVOICEFINE HELPS**: Practical feature tie-in.
- **COMPLIANCE DISCLAIMER**: Standard educational notice.

## Examples
```text
LESSON: How to Calculate Your Store's True Net Profit

THE RETAIL REALITY:
You made Rs 1,00,000 in counter sales this month. But where did the money go?
Gross sales are not your profit. You must subtract cost of goods sold AND daily shop overheads.

THE 3-STEP PROFIT CHECK:
1. Sales Revenue - Wholesale Purchase Cost = Gross Margin.
2. Gross Margin - Daily Shop Overheads (Rent + Electricity + Salaries) = True Net Profit.
3. If you don't track overheads, you might be selling at a loss without realizing it!

HOW INVOICEFINE HELPS:
Use the built-in 'Expense Tracker' to record shop rent, tea, and utility bills. The Dashboard automatically subtracts expenses from sales to show your exact net profit every evening.

DISCLAIMER: Educational information only; not financial or legal advice.
```
