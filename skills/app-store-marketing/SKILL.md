# InvoiceFine App Store Optimization (ASO) Skill

## Purpose
Create high-conversion Google Play Store and mobile app store marketing assets, including screenshot caption banners, short descriptions, full promotional listings, and update changelogs.

## When to Use
- Updating Google Play Store listing graphics and text.
- Writing release notes for app updates.
- Designing promotional banners and feature graphics (1024x500).

## Inputs
- Version number and newly confirmed features.
- Primary search keywords (e.g. GST billing app, retail billing, khata book, offline billing).
- Visual asset constraints.

## Workflow
1. **Short Description**: Draft an impactful 80-character summary focusing on offline billing and business management.
2. **Long Description**: Structure with scannable headers:
   - What InvoiceFine does.
   - 6 Core feature bullet sections (Billing, Khata, Inventory, Printing, Offline, Security).
   - Who should use it (Kirana, Retail, Services).
3. **Screenshot Deck (6–8 Screens)**: Pair real screenshots with bold benefit banners (e.g. "Bill 100% Offline", "Thermal Bluetooth Printing").
4. **Feature Graphic**: Layout concept for the 1024x500 header banner.

## Rules
- Comply strictly with Google Play Store metadata guidelines: no keyword stuffing, no fake ratings, no claims of "#1 Billing App".
- Always use real app screenshots from `assets/screenshots/mobile/`.
- Highlight offline functionality and data privacy prominently.

## Output Format
- **APP TITLE**: InvoiceFine: Billing & Khata
- **SHORT DESCRIPTION**: Under 80 characters.
- **LONG DESCRIPTION**: Structured, bulleted presentation.
- **SCREENSHOT BANNER CONCEPTS**: Headline text and paired screenshot asset per screen.
- **WHAT'S NEW / CHANGELOG**: User-friendly update highlights.

```text
APP TITLE: InvoiceFine: GST Billing & POS
SHORT DESCRIPTION: Fast GST billing, digital customer khata & thermal printing. 100% offline!

STORE SCREENSHOT SEQUENCE:

```

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Conforms to store metadata character limits.
- [ ] No prohibited superlative claims (#1, Best, Government Approved).
- [ ] Paired with real mobile screenshots.
- [ ] Highlights offline capability and ease of use.
