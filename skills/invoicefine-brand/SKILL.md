# InvoiceFine Brand Identity & Voice Skill

## Purpose
Enforce official visual branding, core colors, character traits, and tone guidelines across all generated marketing assets and communications.

## When to Use
- Whenever establishing the visual style of a creative asset.
- When drafting headlines, taglines, or body copy to ensure brand voice alignment.
- When checking color palettes, typography directions, or visual compositions.

## Inputs
- Content objective (Awareness, Education, Conversion).
- Target audience (Kirana, Retail, Wholesaler, Services).
- Medium (Graphic, Video, Print, Web).

## Workflow
1. Check brand color tokens: Crimson Red `#EF3035` (primary CTA/accent) and Slate Charcoal `#171717` (text).
2. Align tone: Honest, practical, modern, and accessible to Indian retail merchants.
3. Verify anti-patterns: Ensure no crypto/neon gradients, fake dashboards, or glassmorphism.
4. Apply white space and clean typography hierarchy.

## Rules
- Use crimson red `#EF3035` purposefully for focal points, highlights, and primary buttons. Do not flood backgrounds with solid red.
- Keep typography clean, high-contrast, and legible on mobile screens.
- Portray Indian businesses with dignity, modern tools, and professional authenticity.

## Output Format
- **Brand Alignment Summary**: Brief statement of how the asset reflects InvoiceFine values.
- **Color Codes**: Explicit hex codes utilized.
- **Tone Profile**: Professional, practical, or conversational tone markers.

## Examples
- *Correct Tone*: "Keep your shop records neat, print thermal receipts in 2 seconds, and track customer dues with complete peace of mind."
- *Incorrect Tone*: "Revolutionize your retail empire with cutting-edge hyper-growth fintech algorithms."

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Primary brand color `#EF3035` applied strategically.
- [ ] No crypto, neon, or flashy fintech aesthetics.
- [ ] Language is clear, humble, and practical.
- [ ] Portrayal of Indian retail environment is authentic.
