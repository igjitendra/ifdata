## 2. Brand Voice & Personality

### Character Traits
- **Practical & Direct**: We address daily billing and inventory problems with clear solutions.
- **Trustworthy**: Financial numbers, tax math, and customer ledgers must be exact and reliable.
- **Approachable**: Friendly and respectful to all merchants, whether running a small village Kirana or a large multi-counter retail showroom.
- **Modern yet Grounded**: Cutting-edge offline SQLite engine wrapped in an accessible, clutter-free mobile and desktop interface.

### Tone Across Languages
- **English**: Simple, professional, actionable. Avoid high-level Silicon Valley buzzwords.
- **Hinglish**: Respectful, conversational, commercial. Mirrors how Indian merchants discuss business at their counter (e.g., *"Hisaab bilkul clear"*, *"Stock khatam hone se pehle alert"*).
- **Hindi**: Grammatically natural, simple vocabulary. Avoid overly heavy Sanskritized words or clumsy machine translations.

## 3. Visual & Creative Direction

### Allowed & Encouraged
- Real Indian retail environments: Kirana shops, mobile accessories stores, garment boutiques, hardware counters.
- Clean physical counters with authentic items: barcode scanners, thermal receipt rolls, card swipe machines, neatly stacked goods.
- Professional device mockups holding authentic InvoiceFine screenshots.
- Generous white space with purposeful crimson red `#EF3035` highlights on primary CTAs.

### Strictly Prohibited Anti-Patterns
- **No Crypto / Web3 Aesthetic**: No holographic gradients, floating coins, or dark neon grids.
- **No Flashy Fintech Fluff**: No glassmorphism bubbles, rocket emojis, or unrealistic futuristic holographic displays.
- **No Fake Dashboards**: Never render fake charts claiming "300% profit increase in 2 days".
- **No Exaggerated Stereotypes**: Portray Indian merchants with dignity, professional agency, and modern business acumen.
