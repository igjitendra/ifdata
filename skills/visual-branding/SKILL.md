# InvoiceFine Visual Branding & Graphic Direction Skill

## Purpose
Enforce graphic design rules, layout grids, typography hierarchies, and color distributions across static banners, social cards, slide decks, and marketing posters.

## When to Use
- Directing graphic designers or creating layout specifications.
- Designing multi-element marketing creatives combining devices, typography, and badges.
- Ensuring visual consistency across campaign collaterals.

## Inputs
- Asset format and canvas dimensions (e.g. 1080x1350 for Instagram 4:5, 1200x628 for Facebook, 1024x500 for Google Play).
- Visual elements required (Screenshot, Headline, CTA, Logo).

## Workflow
1. Establish background: Clean white (`#FFFFFF`) or soft neutral gray (`#F9FAFB`) with subtle modern drop shadows.
2. Position hero asset: Device mockup (phone or monitor) placed slightly off-center or anchored to the bottom.
3. Establish typographic hierarchy:
   - Primary Headline: 48–64px bold, `#171717`.
   - Subheadline / Supporting text: 24–32px medium, `#4B5563`.
   - CTA Pill: High-contrast `#EF3035` button with white bold text.
4. Integrate brand marks: Official InvoiceFine logo from `assets/logo/invoicefine-logo.png` anchored in top corner with safe breathing space.

## Rules
- The primary crimson red `#EF3035` must never cover more than 20% of the total canvas area. Use it exclusively for focal accents, badges, and primary buttons.
- Ensure at least 24px of edge padding on all sides to prevent text clipping on mobile feeds.
- Maintain minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 for all text elements.

## Output Format
- **CANVAS DIMENSIONS & ASPECT RATIO**
- **BACKGROUND SPECIFICATION**
- **TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY**: Font styles, sizes, and colors.
- **ASSET LAYOUT**: Exact placement coordinates and device positioning.
- **COLOR DISTRIBUTION BREAKDOWN**

## Examples
```text
FORMAT: Instagram Portrait Card (1080 x 1350 px, 4:5 Ratio)
BACKGROUND: Subtle vertical gradient from #FFFFFF at top to #F3F4F6 at bottom.

LAYOUT GRID:
- Top 0–350px: Brand logo (top-left, 60px height) + Bold Headline ("Print Thermal Receipts in 2 Taps") in #171717.
- Middle 350–1100px: Angled smartphone mockup holding real screenshot 'assets/screenshots/mobile/mobile-screen-4.png' with subtle 15px Gaussian drop shadow.
- Bottom 1100–1350px: High-contrast crimson button (#EF3035) reading "Download Free • 100% Offline" + Google Play badge.
```

## Quality Checklist
- [ ] Primary red `#EF3035` kept within 20% surface area limit.
- [ ] Strong visual hierarchy from headline to mockup to CTA.
- [ ] Safe padding respected on all canvas boundaries.
- [ ] Real app screenshot properly positioned.
