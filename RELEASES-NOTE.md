# InvoiceFine v1.2.0 — Linux Desktop Release

Welcome to the official **InvoiceFine v1.2.0** desktop release for Linux workstations and retail counter POS terminals.

---

## ⚡ Highlights in v1.2.0

- **Fast POS Counter Billing**: High-speed retail invoicing optimized for keyboard-driven counter operations and USB barcode guns.
- **Automated GST & Non-GST Modes**: One-click tax calculation for intra-state (CGST + SGST) and inter-state (IGST) transactions.
- **Customer & Supplier Ledger**: Integrated digital Khata tracking payments, outstanding balances, and credit histories.
- **Hardware Printing Support**:
  - Direct 58mm (2-inch) and 80mm (3-inch) thermal receipt printing via USB OTG and CUPS.
  - Full-page A4 invoice printing with 5 professional layout styles.
- **100% Offline Architecture**: Complete SQLite database running locally on your device with zero cloud or internet dependency.
- **Phone Backup Sync**: Import and export backup archives seamlessly between Android mobile and Linux desktop.
