## How to Get Help

### 1. Installation & Setup Issues
If you experience difficulties installing the desktop package (`.deb` or `.rpm`) on Linux or configuring the Android app:
- Check the step-by-step guides in [DESKTOP-APP.md](DESKTOP-APP.md) and [MOBILE-APP.md](MOBILE-APP.md).
- Ensure your Linux distribution has `webkit2gtk-4.1` installed.
- When contacting support, please include your Linux distribution name and version (e.g., Ubuntu 24.04, Fedora 40) or Android device model.

### 2. Thermal Printer Configuration
Having trouble printing receipts?
- Review the hardware recommendations in [FAQ.md](FAQ.md).
- Ensure your mobile device has Bluetooth turned on and permission granted.
- On Linux, ensure your user belongs to the `lp` and `dialout` groups (`sudo usermod -aG lp,dialout $USER`).

### 3. Reporting Bugs
Found a bug or an unexpected error? Please email us with:
- The platform you are using (Android version or Linux distribution).
- The exact InvoiceFine version (e.g., `1.2.0`).
- A brief description of what happened and the steps to reproduce the issue.
- A screenshot or photo of the error screen (if applicable).

### 4. Submitting Feature Requests
Have an idea to make InvoiceFine better for your shop or industry? We welcome your suggestions!
Send your feature proposal to [procsctools@gmail.com](mailto:procsctools@gmail.com) with the subject line: `[Feature Request] - Your Suggestion`.

### 5. Billing & Subscription Inquiries
For questions regarding the Pro subscription, receipt lookup, or subscription restoration on new Android devices:
- Contact support at [procsctools@gmail.com](mailto:procsctools@gmail.com) with your Google Play Order ID (`GPA.xxxx-xxxx-xxxx-xxxxx`).
